Lemma 1. If (xn)(x_n)(xn​) is a convergent and increasing sequence, then lim⁡xn\lim x_nlimxn​ is the least upper bound of the set {xn:n∈N}\{x_n: n \in \mathbf{N}\}{xn​:n∈N}
